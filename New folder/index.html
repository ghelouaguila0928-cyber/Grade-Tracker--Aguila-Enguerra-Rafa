About Grade Viewer

A simple, secure way for students and educators to view grades online—anytime, anywhere.

What is Grade Viewer?

Grade Viewer is a lightweight, web-based portal built to make academic results clear and accessible. It provides a single place to check course grades, view grading breakdowns, and understand overall progress without digging through spreadsheets or emails.

Who is it for?

  • Students who want quick, mobile-friendly access to grades and term summaries.
  • Teachers who need a straightforward way to publish grades and keep students informed.
  • Parents/Advisers (optional access) who track academic progress with permission.

What you can do

View per-course grades and overall standing, see grading components (quizzes, assignments, exams), and track term or semester summaries. Built-in term filters and search make it easy to find specific subjects or periods.

How it works

  1. Secure Sign-In: Users log in through a dedicated portal (student or teacher).
  2. Grade Sync: Teachers upload or enter grades; the viewer updates instantly.
  3. Clear Display: Students see organized, readable grade tables and overall calculations.

Privacy & Security

Only authorized users can view grades. Access is protected via account-based authentication, and sensitive information is kept private in accordance with institutional policies.
